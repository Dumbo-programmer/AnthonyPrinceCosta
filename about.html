EDUCATOR · INSTITUTION-BUILDER · ACADEMIC MENTOR

Educator, Institution-Builder, and Academic Mentor

Since joining the Department of Mathematics at St. Joseph Higher Secondary School in 2011, Anthony Prince Costa has been dedicated to transforming how students experience mathematics and higher education. Currently serving as a Lecturer and Honorary Assistant Professor, his approach combines rigorous analytical training with deeply personalized mentorship—helping students look beyond exams to discover intellectual curiosity, resilience, and global ambition.

The classroom and beyond

Mathematics instruction is the centre of the work: over a decade of higher-secondary teaching built on conceptual mastery, proof-writing and creative problem-solving. His own training at Jagannath University, following Notre Dame College and St. Patrick’s Grammar School, supplies the analytical vocabulary—real and complex analysis, algebra, differential equations and modelling—that is tested daily in the classroom.

The aim is not only to cover a syllabus, but to help students ask better questions: what a statement really claims, which assumptions it needs, and how a proof earns its place. Personalized mentorship extends that habit beyond examinations into intellectual curiosity, resilience and global ambition.

Pioneering Olympiad culture & global representation

Early in his career at St. Joseph, he partnered with Dipak Sarkar—a prominent figure in the Bangladesh Mathematical Olympiad Committee—to co-found the Josephite Math Club (JMC). Under his sustained leadership as Chief Moderator, JMC has evolved into a premier hub for mathematical enrichment, hosting workshops, peer-mentorship pipelines, and major national events, including bringing the Bangladesh Mathematical Olympiad (BdMO) National Rounds to the St. Joseph campus.

Beyond domestic initiatives, he regularly leads and chaperones student delegations abroad each year to represent Bangladesh at prestigious international mathematics and science competitions—giving young scholars firsthand exposure to global academic standards and cross-cultural collaboration.

University admissions counseling & global pathways

Recognizing that academic excellence deserves the right global platform, he also serves as a dedicated counselor and mentor for international university admissions. Over the years, he has guided numerous students through the intricacies of competitive applications, personal storytelling, and academic positioning—successfully mentoring scholars into Harvard University and top-tier U.S. Liberal Arts Colleges (LACs), as well as supporting both undergraduate and postgraduate applicants in securing admissions and financial aid at their dream institutions worldwide.
