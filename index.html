01 // ABOUT

About the work

Since 2011, Anthony Prince Costa has taught mathematics at St. Joseph Higher Secondary School, Dhaka—building a practice around conceptual mastery, proof-writing and creative problem-solving.

He co-founded the Josephite Math Club with Dipak Sarkar, hosts BdMO National Rounds on campus, mentors annual international delegations, and advises students applying to Harvard and top U.S. Liberal Arts Colleges.

ST. JOSEPH HIGHER SECONDARY SCHOOL · DHAKA DEPT. OF MATHEMATICS

Since joining St. Joseph Higher Secondary School in 2011, Anthony Prince Costa has spent 15+ years transforming how students experience mathematics and higher education.

In his lectures, abstract symbolic expressions are consistently anchored to their geometric and physical meaning—inviting students to appreciate why a theorem holds from first principles rather than relying on rote mechanical procedures.
